# licence-diff

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose and users

Independent local developer/legal engineering product. The CLI creates a source-linked
analysis package; the responsible reviewer inspects, corrects or disputes findings and
exports a reviewed deliverable. Product boundaries and acceptance criteria are in README.md.

## Constraints

The user authorised autonomous completion. Inputs remain local; sample matters and
demonstration decisions are synthetic. Review UI is a self-contained offline HTML
report, an implementation choice made within that brief. No accounts, external AI
services, live matter integrations or dependence on the legal-engineering repositories.
